## Special Campaign: 7-Day Growth Challenge (Week 3 or 4)

**Goal**: Drive engagement, create user-generated content, build community

### Structure:
- **Days 1-7**: Daily prompts for users to complete sessions + share insights
- **Prize**: Free Pro upgrade for top 3 most insightful shares
- **Hashtag**: #FocusGrowChallenge

### Daily Challenge Prompts:

**Day 1**: "Complete your first session this week. What's one pattern you notice in your work?"
**Day 2**: "Session 2: What would change if you approached today's task from a different angle?"
**Day 3**: "Session 3: What skill are you using today that you want to evolve?"
**Day 4**: "Session 4: If you had to teach someone what you're working on, what would you realize you don't fully understand?"
**Day 5**: "Session 5: What's one assumption you're making that you haven't questioned?"
**Day 6**: "Session 6: How is today's work building a capability you'll use in 6 months?"
**Day 7**: "Session 7: Reflect on the week. What's one mindset shift you experienced?"

### Social Posts:
- **Kickoff** (Day 0): Announce challenge, explain rules, invite participation
- **Daily Check-Ins** (Days 1-7): Quote best user shares, encourage participation
- **Wrap-Up** (Day 8): Announce winners, share insights, recap transformations

**Expected Results**:
- 20-30 user participants
- 100+ social mentions
- User-generated testimonials
- Increased session completion rate

## Content Creation Workflow (Sustainable)

### Weekly Batch Work (2-3 hours every Sunday):
1. **Review Metrics** (30 min): Analyze week's data, identify trends
2. **Plan Content** (45 min): Outline posts for upcoming week based on themes
3. **Create Graphics** (60 min): Design 3-5 visuals in Canva (quote graphics, carousels)
4. **Write Drafts** (45 min): Draft key posts (threads, LinkedIn long-forms)

### Daily Execution (30-45 min/day):
1. **Post Content** (10 min): Publish scheduled posts, adjust timing
2. **Engage** (20 min): Respond to comments, participate in discussions
3. **Monitor** (15 min): Track metrics, note what's working

### Tools:
- **Canva**: Visual content (templates saved for brand consistency)
- **Notion**: Content calendar, idea tracking, metrics dashboard
- **Buffer/Typefully**: Scheduling (optional, can post manually)
- **Loom/CleanShot X**: Screen recordings for demos
- **Google Sheets**: Metrics tracking

## Next Steps: Month 2+ Strategy

**If Month 1 Validates** (>100 users, >15% D30 retention):
- Launch freemium pricing ($7/month Pro)
- Scale content production
- Test paid acquisition channels
- Build advanced features (integrations, analytics)

**If Month 1 Plateaus** (growth stalls, retention drops):
- Pivot marketing messaging
- Target different audience segment
- Add viral features (referral program, social sharing)
- Interview churned users for insights

**If Month 1 Fails** (<50 users, <10% retention):
- Post-mortem analysis
- Pivot to simpler product (Pomodoro timer only)
- Return to ideation with learnings

**Principle**: Month 1 is about proving the concept scales. Month 2+ is about proving it's a business.
