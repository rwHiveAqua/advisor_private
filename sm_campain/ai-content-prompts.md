## Brand Consistency Guidelines for All Graphics

### Colors (Always Use):
- Deep Ocean Blue (#0A2E4D): Primary text, backgrounds
- Insight Cyan (#00B4D8): Accents, highlights, gradients
- Transformation Teal (#06D6A0): CTAs, success states, icons
- Neutral Light (#F8F9FA): Backgrounds, cards

### Typography:
- Headings: Bold, sans-serif (Inter, Montserrat, or Poppins)
- Body: Regular/Medium sans-serif
- Sizes: Large for impact, adequate line-height for readability

### Visual Style:
- Minimalist, generous whitespace
- Modern SaaS aesthetic (Notion, Linear, Stripe inspiration)
- Clean, professional, not playful or startup-y
- Gradients suggest forward movement, growth
- Icons: Line-based, simple, transformation teal

### Don'ts:
- ❌ Busy backgrounds or patterns
- ❌ Stock photos of people (unless testimonials)
- ❌ Generic motivational imagery (sunsets, mountain peaks)
- ❌ Cluttered text layouts
- ❌ Off-brand colors or fonts
